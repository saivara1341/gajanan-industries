GAJANAN INDUSTRIESINDUSTRY IMAGE · COMING SOON

Established in 1969 from a visionary rice milling unit in Nizamabad, Shree Gajanan Industries has grown into one of India’s premier non-Basmati rice producers. Our work pairs agrarian wisdom with Swiss precision and uncompromising integrity.

04 / MILESTONES

Milestones that
shape every grain.

1969

Started with a conventional rice-processing unit in Nizamabad.

1999

Installed a fully automated Bühler paddy-processing plant, a first for non-Basmati rice in South India.

2008

Installed a second automated paddy-processing plant operated with SCADA technology.

2012–18

Earned ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, Organic, GFSI and SEDEX SMETA certifications.

2020–21

Started backward integration with Farm Chakra and was recognised as a Star Export House.
