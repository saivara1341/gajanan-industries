ACT 01 · AGRARIAN ROOTS & SINGLE-ORIGIN HARVEST

Grounded in fertile soil.
Nurtured grain by grain.

ACT 02 · SWISS AUTOMATION VANGUARD

South India’s First Bühler Plant.
Computerized 16 TPH SCADA.

STAGE 01

Intelligent De-Stoning

Multi-frequency vibratory sieves and high-gradient magnets remove 100% of chaff and field stones.

STAGE 02

Silo Conditioning

Closed-loop humidity ventilation stabilizes moisture uniformly between 12% and 13%.

Differential rubber rollers gently peel outer husks without bruising or fracturing the delicate core.

Bichromatic optical CCD cameras scan 2,000 FPS to eject discolored or chalky kernels.

Auto Tamper Bagging

Robotic baggers seal 5kg, 10kg, 25kg, and 30kg sacks with nitrogen flush for 425-day freshness.
