02 / ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE

Swiss Bühler SCADA.
Zero grain defect.

Installed in 1999 as South India’s first automated paddy processing facility for non-Basmati rice, our plant pairs high-precision optical laser sorting with electronically controlled conditioning silos.

STAGE 01

Multi-Stage Pre-Cleaning

Heavy-duty magnetic separators and aspirators eliminate all chaff, dust, and micro-stones before milling starts.

STAGE 02

Electronic Conditioning

Closed-loop moisture sensors stabilize internal grain moisture between 12-13% to prevent breakage.

STAGE 03

Gentle Friction Polishing

Multi-pass Bühler abrasive rolls preserve the vital aleurone layer and maintain the kernel's natural aroma.

STAGE 04

Optical Laser Sortex

2000 FPS bichromatic cameras detect and eject discolored, pecked, or chalky grains with precision air blasts.

STAGE 05

Nitrogen-Flushed Bagging

Automated bagging lines seal every 5kg, 10kg, 25kg, and 30kg sack in tamper-evident laminated woven poly.
