1969

The Seed of Enterprise

Founded with a humble conventional rice processing unit in Nizamabad operating at 1 metric ton per hour, anchored by agrarian integrity.

🌱

1980–1993

Rooting & Modernization

Installed a pioneering 5 TPH fully automated Bühler paddy-processing facility — an unprecedented industrial milestone in the non-Basmati segment.
