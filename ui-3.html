02 / SWISS BÜHLER PRECISION

SCADA automation.
Zero grain compromise.

In 1999, Shree Gajanan Industries introduced South India’s first automated Bühler milling plant. Today, our electronically conditioned facility processes up to 16 metric tons per hour with optical laser sorting.

16 TPH SCADA Capacity

Closed-loop digital controls dynamically tune dehulling, de-stoning, and polishing to preserve kernel integrity without fracturing.

Micro-Optical Laser Sorting

High-speed bichromatic cameras scan individual grains, rejecting discolored kernels, chalky grains, and impurities at 2,000 FPS.

Controlled Conditioning

Electronically regulated silos stabilize moisture between 12-13%, locking in grain sweetness and ensuring uniform boiling behavior.

Global Export Certified

Audited by USFDA, SEDEX SMETA, ISO 22000, and FSSC 22000. Supplying private labels and retailers across 40+ countries.
