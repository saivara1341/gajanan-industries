01 / WHO WE ARE

CRAFTING
PURITY IN
EVERY GRAIN.

Established in 1969 from a visionary rice milling unit in Nizamabad, Shree Gajanan Industries has grown into one of India's premier non-Basmati rice producers. Guided by uncompromised integrity, state-of-the-art Swiss precision, and over five decades of agrarian wisdom, we bring perfection to culinary tables worldwide.

06 / MILESTONES

STILL
GROWING.

1969

Started with a conventional rice-processing unit in Nizamabad.

1999

Installed a fully automated Buhler paddy-processing plant, a first for non-Basmati rice in South India.

2008

Installed a second automated paddy-processing plant operated with SCADA technology from Buhler.

2012–18

Earned ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, Organic, GFSI and SEDEX SMETA certifications.

2020–21

Started backward integration with Farm Chakra and was recognised as a Star Export House.
